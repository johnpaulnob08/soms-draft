THE RE-REGISTRATION SYSTEM

Please read all instructions below before accomplishing the requirements.

ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Every year, student organizations need to update their records with the Office of Student Affairs – Student Activities and Leadership Development (OSA-SACDEV) by accomplishing the re-registration requirements. This requirement is crucial to the status of the organization as it will be the basis for enlisting recognized student organizations who will be allowed to operate for the academic year. Only the President or the duly authorized representative of the organization is allowed to submit the requirements.

The following are the benefits and privileges of recognized student organizations:

  • Use of the name of the university
  • Use of university facilities
  • Raising funds
  • Sponsoring speakers and public performances
  • Promotion of activities inside the university
  • Support from the Student Organization Fund (SOF) or Parents-Teachers' Association (PTA) Fund
  • Assistance in terms of planning and implementing projects/activities
  • Interventions on conflict management and other organizational concerns
  • Support from the university in regional and national assemblies and conferences
  • Participation in leadership and formation programs of the university

SPECIFIC INSTRUCTIONS
  1. Visit www.xu.edu.ph/osa and click "Org Registration".
  2. Fill out the forms. Please carefully follow the given instructions.
  3. Upload the following files:
    • Current edition of org constitution
    • Current org logo/seal
  4. For Form B-5.1 Moderator's Profile, please send it to your moderator. Ask your moderator to accomplish the form and send it back to you. You will be the one to submit it online.
  5. As much as possible, gather all the required documents first before attempting to access the re-registration form and submit all requirements together.
  6. Submit accomplished forms through www.xu.edu.ph/osa. Click the "Org Registration" tab. Please upload all accomplished forms through the portal. Do not send them via email. Be sure to affix e-signatures of the required signatories before uploading. No need to submit printed copies.
  7. For grade requirement, please gather screenshots of the 2nd semester grades of ALL OFFICERS as listed on Form B-3. Compile the screenshots in one word/PDF file (short size) before submitting online.
  8. Please rename files according to the following format:
    • ORG ACRONYM_Constitution
    • ORG ACRONYM_Logo
  9. Submit all requirements on or before 30 AUGUST 2026. (Academic Year 2026–2027)
  10. OSA-SACDEV will evaluate your re-registration requirements before the granting of recognition. Only those included in the list of recognized organizations shall be allowed to operate for Academic Year 2026–2027.
ORGANIZATION MODERATOR

Moderators play a very important role in student organizations since they help you plan, monitor, implement and evaluate your projects. They also play vital role in helping you address various organizational concerns and attain your success. It is important that you consider the personal expertise of your moderator and establish a relationship that is built on trust and support.

Duties and responsibilities of moderators:

  • Provide assistance to students in planning, monitoring, implementing and evaluating projects
  • Advance participation in student activities and encourage students to deliver their duties and responsibilities
  • Assist in coordinating inter-organization projects and activities or coordinating with other offices
  • Promote alliance-building with other organizations
  • Act as liaison officer between the organization and the administration through OSA-SACDEV
  • Attend meetings and other organization activities when necessary
  • Countersign all written reports and communications of the organization
  • Help promote leadership and social awareness
  • Help enhance academic discipline
  • Motivate students to be competent men and women for others

Modification During the Hybrid Learning Mode

  1. Student orgs may either retain their moderator from last year or nominate another. For the latter, the student org may submit only one nominee who will be deliberated upon by OSA-SACDEV.
  2. Basic qualifications for moderator:
    • College councils – any full-time faculty or staff member from your college
    • Co-curricular organizations – any full-time faculty member from your own department
    • Extra-curricular organizations – any full-time faculty, formator or staff member from the university
    • Not a moderator of any other organization
  3. Please consider the skills, knowledge and expertise of your nominee. It is important that they are supportive or have background aligned with the nature, scope, field, cause or advocacy of your organization.
